01Why this room

Two questions everyowner is quietly asking

“What is actually worth using. And how do I avoid getting burned trying.”

Those are the two real questions. Not “what is a large language model.” The owners I talk to are past that. They want to know where AI saves them real hours, where it quietly costs them, and what a person running a small business should do on Monday morning.

The honest answer does not come from a vendor with a demo to sell. It comes from someone who spent ten years training people on new tools and now runs his own business on the AI he is talking about.

That is the room this workshop builds. Plain English. No hype. A live system you can watch work, and a thing you build yourself before you leave.

03Who this is for

For people who carrythe whole thing

Owners, founders, operators. Small team or no team. You make the calls and you live with them.

This is for you if

  • You run a business and AI keeps coming up, but no one has shown you what actually matters for it.
  • You are short on time and want one thing you can use, not a survey of forty tools.
  • You learn by doing, with someone in the room when you get stuck.
  • You want a straight answer about where AI helps and where it bites.

This is not for you if

  • You want a deep technical course on building models from scratch.
  • You are looking for a passive lecture to sit through with your phone out.
  • You already run AI deeply across your operation and want advanced tuning.
