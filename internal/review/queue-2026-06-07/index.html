Post 2 - Method not tooling (no CTA)

LinkedIn

For ten years my Drive sat one weekend away from done. New apps, new folder schemes, new year's resolutions. Nothing held. Not because I was lazy. Because somewhere in those folders sat family records I could not afford to lose, and I had no safe way to move anything. Every move was a gamble. So for a decade I made none. Here is the part I did not see at the time. Buried in that same chaos were years of my own research on Theory of Constraints, the discipline of finding the one real bottleneck in a system. I had the whole framework. I just never pointed it at my own desk. The bottleneck was never the files. It was the missing method. Two rules changed everything. Move, never delete: everything goes to a labeled holding area first, so deletion becomes a separate, calmer decision later. Dry-run first: see the entire plan before a single file moves. Once every move was reversible, the fear had nothing left to hold onto. What have you been trying to out-tool instead of out-think?
