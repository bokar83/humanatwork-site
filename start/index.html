Your first Safe Week of AI usage
  1. Monday

    Read the rules

    Find your company's AI policy. Sign in with your work account, not a personal one.

    15 to 20 min
  2. Tuesday

    Try one safe thing

    Nothing from your job goes in. Ask it to explain something you are new to.

    15 to 20 min
  3. Wednesday

    Set your context

    A short note about your work, kept in a file you own, pasted into a new chat.

    15 to 20 min
  4. Thursday

    Practice on a made-up task

    Then check the answer the way you would check a colleague's work.

    15 to 20 min
  5. Friday

    Look back

    Three lines: what saved time, what needed fixing, what you will not use it for. Then start the job audit worksheet.

    15 to 20 min
