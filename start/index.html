What gets in the way

People often do not call it fear. Said out loud, it sounds like this:

  • "What if I break something?" The first week asks you to send nothing, delete nothing, and put nothing from your job in.
  • "I do not know where to start." Monday has one job: find your company's rules.
  • "I do not know what to do with it, or what it could do for me." One small task a day, and a list of the places people find real time.
  • "What if I cannot do what I am supposed to do with it?" You do not need to be technical. You need to check the work, and the kit shows you how.
  • "Am I even allowed?" The policy check comes first.

What is inside

  • A risk ladder. What is safe to try first, what takes care, and what AI should not touch.
  • A policy check. Where to find your employer's rules and what to ask if there are none.
  • A context note. A short file you own, so every chat starts from your work.
  • A verification habit. How to catch a wrong answer before it leaves your desk.
  • A first week, day by day. 15 to 20 minutes a day, small steps.
  • Beyond email. Where people find the real time.

Who it is for

Anyone who works for someone else, has an AI tool in front of them or is about to, and wants to start with confidence instead of guessing. You do not have to be technical, and you do not have to know everything first.

General information for working people. Not legal, compliance, or HR advice. Your employer's policy decides what you may do.
